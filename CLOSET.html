Closet Extension — Wiring

Under-stairs closet strip. Standalone from the main 5-bay Apollo system: no ESP32, no PWM, no dimming. Door open → light on, door closed → light off. Both channels tied at the strip, always full → ~4000 K mix.
Companion to BACKPLATE.html  ·  See apollo_lighting_control_system_summary.md § Closet extension

1 Behavior

A magnetic reed switch on the closet door drives a 24 V DC relay coil. The relay's NC contact carries the strip's +24 V rail. With the door closed, the magnet holds the reed closed → the coil is energized → NC opens → strip off. With the door open, the reed opens → coil drops out → NC closes → strip on. Behavior matches a fridge light; the relay does the current-carrying, so the reed only ever sees the coil's small pull-in current (well under 100 mA).

4 Reed polarity note

The TAKHRWOD MC-31B is a Normally-Open reed: contact closes when the magnet is aligned. Wanted behavior is the opposite — closed door should mean off. The relay's NC output does that inversion for free:

DoorReedRelay coilNC contactStrip
Closed (magnet aligned)ClosedEnergizedOpenOFF
OpenOpenDropped outClosedON
If IN floats when the reed is open: some opto-isolated boards leave the IN pin unterminated in HIGH-trigger mode, which can chatter the coil. If that happens on the bench, add a 10 kΩ pull-down from IN to GND — reed closed still pulls IN firmly to +24 V, reed open holds IN at 0 V.

5 Bench-test procedure

  1. Wire the DC rails to the relay module first, with no strip attached. Barrel jack + → relay VCC. Barrel jack − → relay GND.
  2. Wire the reed as the IN driver. Tap a second lead off the +24 V rail to one reed terminal; the other reed terminal to relay IN.
  3. Plug in the brick. Sweep the magnet toward and away from the reed body: you should hear the relay's coil click on magnet aligned and click off magnet away. The board's power LED lights on plug-in; the relay LED tracks the coil state.
  4. Verify with a multimeter (continuity mode) across relay COM ↔ NC: open with magnet aligned, closed with magnet away. If reversed, re-check the trigger jumper — LOW trigger inverts the sense.
  5. Now attach the strip. Cut a ~30 cm test length from reel stock; tie warm− and cool− together at the strip pad, run that common return to barrel jack −. Run the strip's +24 V pad through relay COM → NC (i.e. relay NC is the pad-side wire; COM taps the +24 V rail).
  6. Sweep the magnet again. Magnet away → strip full. Magnet aligned → strip dark. Expect ~1.25 A draw on the full run when lit; the brick's LED shouldn't dim or the case get hot.
Before final install: confirm the reed halves land within the manufacturer's activation gap when the door is fully shut (typically 15–25 mm for surface-mount reeds — verify on the MC-31B datasheet). A too-wide gap leaves the light stuck on with the door closed; a too-narrow one is rarely a problem but the switch may buzz on a sagging door.

6 What lives outside this diagram

  • AC-side rocker as the master kill, upstream of the wall outlet — a plain panel-mount rocker in a shallow box. Not shown; it just gates line power to the brick.
  • Mechanical mounting — ITROLLE clips onto each joist face, one strip per joist, strain-relieved into the junction box via the two glands.
  • Main-room control system. Zero shared wiring, zero shared power. If the main enclosure is off or being serviced, the closet still works.
